# Qualification rubric

Grade reads each company's website and assigns one category. Keep `ICP`; drop the rest; hold `unclear` for a human look.

| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ICP | Matches icp.md: the firm type, the size band, the geography, and does the workflow by hand |
| agency | Serves the ICP rather than being it |
| saas | Sells software, often to the ICP |
| freelancer | One person, no firm |
| enterprise | Above the size band, buys through procurement |
| wrong-geo | Outside the target geography or language |
| dead-site | Site down, parked, or last updated years ago |
| unclear | Not enough on the site to decide |

## Signals that say ICP

- (what the homepage says about what they do, in their words)
- (the services list, the team page size, the register number)

## Signals that say no

- (a client logo wall of the ICP means agency)
- (a pricing page with plans means saas)

## Reason column

Every graded row carries `category_reason`: one sentence quoting the site. The user audits the filter from that column.
