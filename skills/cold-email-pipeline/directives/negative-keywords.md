# Negative keywords

Words in a company name, headline or website that mean "not the ICP". Applied at search time where possible, and again in Grade.

## Drop on sight

- agency, agence, consulting, consultant, advisory
- software, saas, platform, app
- coach, coaching, training, formation
- freelance, independent contractor (unless the ICP is freelancers)
- franchise, group, holding (when the ICP is independent firms)
- recruiter, recruitment, staffing

## Hold for review (unclear)

- (words that are sometimes the ICP and sometimes a lookalike; Grade reads the site)

## Wrong geography

- (countries, regions or languages that never convert)
